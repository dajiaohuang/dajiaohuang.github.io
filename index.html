How I work

Interfaces on top. Contracts underneath.

{ }

Make authority explicit

Separate the model from the source of truth. Every write path should reveal who may act, against which revision, and with what scope.

↺

Design for maintenance

Verification, rollback, observability, and handoff are product features. A shipped system begins a maintenance relationship.

≠

Keep evidence distinct

A source, an inference, and a polished claim are not interchangeable. Good tools keep the path between them inspectable.
